# P14 Experiment Report: Original-Preserving Geometric Raster Evaluation (T45)

## Executive Summary
- **Experiment ID**: P14
- **Task ID**: T45 (pdf-t45)
- **Status**: `rejected_experiment`
- **Disposition**: `rejected_experiment`
- **Recommendation**: Reject automatic raster deskew / reconstruction; retain original PDF and rendered rasters immutable.

## Candidate & Baseline
- **Baseline**: Unmodified render OCR over native PDFium canonical raster.
- **Candidate**: Bounded affine deskew and strip registration with full inverse transform chain.

## Evaluation Criteria & Findings
1. **Clean Control Corruption (F06, F07, F15)**:
   - On already-aligned clean pages and ruled forms, any global or local deskew estimator suffers from line-orientation ambiguity.
   - Bilinear or bicubic rotation resampling blurs 1-pixel rules and introduces subpixel coordinate drift.
   - Per acceptance contract: "Any lost registration or clean corruption rejects". This failure mode is fatal for the candidate.

2. **Inverse Transform Chain (I02)**:
   - While mathematical inverse matrix computation satisfies orthogonal identity precision (`\le 10^{-9}`), physical raster pixel interpolation is inherently lossy and irreversible once sampled.
   - Inkflip strictly enforces byte and geometry immutability (I01, I02).

3. **No PDF Mutation or Synthesis**:
   - Synthesizing or rewriting modified PDFs is forbidden. The candidate was evaluated as a pure reading transform without PDF reconstruction.

## Conclusion
P14 concludes with formal rejection of automatic raster deskew. No production candidate is promoted. All raw evaluation metrics and negative outcome evidence are preserved in `artifacts/P14/result.json`.
