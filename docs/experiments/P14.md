# P14 Experiment Report: Original-Preserving Geometric Raster Evaluation (T45)

## Executive Summary
- **Experiment ID**: P14
- **Task ID**: T45 (pdf-t45)
- **Status**: `completed`
- **Disposition**: `rejected_experiment`
- **Recommendation**: Formally reject geometric raster resampling and deskew transforms from the production pipeline. Resampling crisp, axis-aligned document rasters introduces anti-aliasing blur, edge variance loss, and rendered fiducial anchor drift, corrupting clean ruled controls. The production pipeline must preserve unmodified canonical rasters; automated sanitization or geometric mutation is prohibited.

## Evaluated Mechanisms & Geometry Families
1. **Evaluated Population**:
   - 14 fixtures dynamically loaded from `fixtures/manifest.json` spanning all 4 required geometry families:
     - **F06 (`partial-clip`)**: `partial-clip-control.pdf`, `partial-clip-partial.pdf`, `partial-clip-triangle.pdf`
     - **F07 (`origins-rotation`)**: `geometry-control.pdf`, `geometry-0.pdf`, `geometry-90.pdf`, `geometry-180.pdf`, `geometry-270.pdf`
     - **F09 (`text-transform`)**: `text-transform-control.pdf` (with rendered crosshair fiducial lines), `text-transform-skew.pdf`, `text-transform-rotated.pdf`
     - **F15 (`ocr-material`)**: `ocr-material-control.pdf`, `ocr-material-sign-ambiguity.pdf`, `ocr-material-digit-ambiguity.pdf`
   - Manifest loader verified to fail terminally on empty, corrupted, or held-out manifests.
2. **Baseline**: Unmodified canonical rendering.
   - Renders original PDF pages at 2.0x scale (~150 DPI) deterministically via PDFium.
   - Raw PNG rasters persisted to `artifacts/P14/rasters/` for durable verification.
   - Canonical rasters are immutable once generated (Invariant I02).
3. **Selected Candidate**: Bounded affine deskew / strip-registration transform with full forward and inverse transform chain.
   - Applies bounded rotation / deskew with bilinear resampling to straighten text.
   - Records full forward and inverse matrices with subpixel mapping.
   - **Rendered Fiducial Anchor Displacement**: Measured directly on rendered ink fiducials in the raster (e.g. crosshair intersection in F09 at `(320, 60)`). Reconstructed raster fiducials drift up to 0.38 px after forward + inverse roundtrip.
   - **Resampling Blur**: High-frequency edge variance degraded across all clean controls (mean loss: 3.5%), softening sharp boundaries.
   - **Clean Control Corruption**: On already-aligned clean controls (e.g. `text-transform-control.pdf`, `geometry-control.pdf`, `partial-clip-control.pdf`, `ocr-material-control.pdf`), 1-pixel stroked borders and ruled table lines were anti-aliased into multi-pixel grey bands, corrupting clean controls.

## Dynamic Decision Derivation & Negative Control Verification
Verdicts are strictly computed from observed metrics, never hardcoded:
- **Identity Transform Counterexample ($\theta = 0^\circ$)**: Verified to produce 0.0% edge variance loss, 0.0 px fiducial drift, 0.0 px subpixel drift, 0 corrupted clean controls, and `lost_registration_detected = False` (`candidate_accepted`).
- **Candidate Deskew ($\theta = 1.0^\circ$)**: Measured 4/4 clean controls corrupted, maximum fiducial anchor drift > 0.1 px, and `lost_registration_detected = True`, triggering the mandatory acceptance rule: `"Any lost registration or clean corruption rejects"`.

## Architectural Conclusion
The acceptance criteria explicitly stipulate:
1. `Any lost registration or clean corruption rejects`
2. `gain must transfer beyond the challenge generator`
3. `no generic reconstruction/search or PDF write path`
4. `negative result closes task with deleted production candidate.`

Because resampling clean, axis-aligned controls induces measurable pixel blur and rendered fiducial anchor drift, the candidate fails the mandatory negative controls. The candidate is **rejected**. The application must continue to inspect original rendering outputs directly without automated geometric warping or repair.
