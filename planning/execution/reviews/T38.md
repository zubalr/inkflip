## Required disposition

Write `artifacts/tasks/T38/review.md`: reviewed commit, reviewer/session identity, commands actually executed, test counts and skips, source/fixture/model identities, issues with severity and reproduction, and accept/request-changes/blocked. Explicitly label inspection-only areas. A material assertion without evidence is not accepted.

Use the task's proposal procedure for plan conflicts. The integrator repeats affected acceptance on the merge result; branch-local success does not certify integration. Preserve original bytes and rejected-experiment receipts. Rollback reverts candidate changes, never rewrites a baseline to erase a failure.
