# ADR 010 — Six curated examples, full release behind capability gates

Status: **accepted planning default**, not a claim of implemented production behavior.

## Decision
Use Inkflip as configurable provisional identity. Select a document stage with evidence slip, progressive details and six concise original example cards. The amount example is generated from real mapping bytes; preparation is visible. Full browser, native, regression, accessibility and deployment scope is required.

## Why this decision and not the obvious alternative
The attached research’s customer-quota or small-MVP investment conditions do not govern this build. Usability studies improve the interface, not decide whether the owner may finish a portfolio artifact. Publish only measured metrics and actual agent-development history.

## Verification and fallback branch
G1 proves one honest end-to-end slice but is not the end of the project. G2–G5 close all downstream scope. Failed optional experiments are complete when documented and removed/default-disabled; essential safety, geometry and privacy failures block release, not quietly narrow truth claims.

## Change control
Submit a proposal with affected schemas, consumers, assets, tests, licensing and rollback. Contract owner and integrator approve before dependent branches rebase. Do not ship both competing architectures or change fixture expectations to disguise a behavioral regression.
