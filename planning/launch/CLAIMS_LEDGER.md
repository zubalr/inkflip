# Public claims and required proof

Planning readiness and product readiness are separate. **No completed application, customer adoption, full product benchmark or deployment is claimed by this package.** The literal native amount proof and planning utility results have actual receipts; everything else needs the listed implementation evidence before publication.

## Release review rule

T49/T50 tag each README/site/video/social/case-study claim with a receipt or explicitly qualitative design wording. T55 rejects claims based only on a spec or stale branch. Numerical public claims include metric definition, data/split, sample denominator, exact build and environment, failure/skip treatment and whether synthetic. Avoid the phrase “what AI sees”: name the actual reader and version.

The earlier `mib-intake` 137.89/150 result is explicitly in-sample historical context, not new product accuracy. Its globally fitted path calibration caveat stays intact. The targeted PR inventory is16 records at mixed inspection depths, not89 newly audited independent solutions.
